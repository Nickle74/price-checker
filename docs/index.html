      How it works

      The round

      Ten bottles, drawn at random. You see the brand, the expression, the size and the proof — never the price.

      The scoring

      Each bottle is worth up to 1,000 points, scored on how far off you are as a percentage — so a $5 miss on a $20 bottle costs you the same as a $50 miss on a $200 one.

        The daily

        Every day draws one fixed set of ten bottles from the date itself, so everyone opens the app to the same round. One attempt, then share the grid and compare. It resets at midnight, your time.

        Streaks

        Land within 15% and you start a streak. Every bottle after the first in a streak adds a +50 bonus, up to +250.

        Learn

        The same shelf, laid out instead of hidden. Pick one size, then any categories and tiers, and read the ladder cheapest to dearest with each brand’s 9-litre cases beside it. The gaps are the lesson: two bottles a dollar apart can be twenty times apart on volume.

        Practice

        Five bottles from one category and one size, shuffled. Drag them into price order, cheapest on the left, and check. No points and no clock — it is the ladder from Learn with the prices taken away.

        Where the prices come from

        Average retail price per item, straight from the distribution file.
